FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the stock change?

Yes. Stock updates constantly with new shipments from Germany. Message us on WhatsApp for today's availability.

Of course. You are welcome during our opening hours at any of the three branches.

Do you offer service?

Yes. Our team offers service and repairs for German household appliances.

Where are the branches?

We have three branches: Yzberisht, Mëzez and Vaqarr. See addresses and maps in the Branches section.

How can I ask about a price?

Call us or message us on WhatsApp with the model you want and we'll send the price right away.
