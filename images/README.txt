B.O. BEOLA — IMAGES FOLDER
==========================

Put your real photos here. The website looks for these file names:

  hero.jpg   → The big background photo at the top (homepage hero).
               Use your BEST, cleanest photo of the warehouse or a row of
               washing machines. Wide/landscape works best.
               Recommended size: about 1920 x 1080 pixels (landscape).

The site already works without any photo (a clean dark background shows
instead), so you can add photos whenever you are ready.

PHOTO TIPS (from your brief):
  • Crop photos straight.
  • Make them a little brighter and cleaner.
  • Avoid messy images as the main hero photo.
  • Blur any visible car license plates.
  • Keep files reasonably small (under ~400 KB) so the site loads fast.
    Free tool: https://squoosh.app  (drag a photo in, export as JPG).

HOW TO ADD MORE PHOTOS LATER (e.g. inside product cards or about section):
  1. Drop the image file into this "images" folder.
  2. Tell me (or your developer) which section it belongs to and I'll place it.
